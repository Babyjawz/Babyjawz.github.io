Fast hand reference

For the numbered stick rankings used in this trainer, strongest to weakest:

1. Pairs

2. Six named combinations

Memorise these exact six, in order. They beat all ordinary points.

3. Ordinary points

Rule: Add the two stick numbers and use only the final digit. Example: 7 + 8 = 15 → 5 points. Pairs and named combinations take priority.

Perfect Nine: a nine-point ordinary hand, the strongest of its group.
