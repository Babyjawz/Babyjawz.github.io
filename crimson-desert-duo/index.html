Crimson Desert — Duo Hands

Learn the hands in three groups. Certain named combinations beat ordinary hands regardless of their total.

1. Hand rankings — strongest to weakest

Pairs — strongest group

Two matching numbers. 10+10 is the strongest ordinary hand, then 9+9 down to 1+1.

Six named combinations — next group

Learn these in the exact order shown. All beat ordinary points.

Ordinary points — lowest group

Add both numbers and keep only the last digit. 9 points is best; 0 is worst.

2. Conditional special hands

These have exceptions to the usual rankings. Confirm their precise conditions using View Hands in the game.
