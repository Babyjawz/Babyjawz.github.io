Craft

Craft is a desktop crafting-recipe editor for Skyrim Special Edition and Anniversary Edition. It reads the mods in your active Mod Organizer 2 profile and lets you decide which recipes appear in crafting menus, when they become visible, and what ingredients they consume.

MO2 launch hotfix — 30 October 2026: The initial Craft.exe download could close immediately when launched through MO2. Use the corrected launch-hotfix package and preserve app/decisions.json when updating. The repaired app has been checked with MO2's hooks active and loaded the intended recipe catalog. See Troubleshooting.

As a modlist grows, its crafting stations can fill with recipes you rarely use or only want to see when you have relevant materials. Craft gives you a way to curate that collection: hide unwanted entries, add ingredient-based visibility rules, and adjust costs across a whole mod or one recipe at a time.

Shape your crafting menus#

Browse recipes grouped by their source mod, select several collections together, and narrow the list by station, recipe identifiers or existing decisions. Inspect an entry to see its ingredients, origin and winning plugin before choosing what to change.

Keep a recipe under its source requirements, hide it from crafting, or add a rule that reveals it when you carry a chosen number of different ingredient types. The result can be a smaller, more useful menu that reflects the content you want to make.

Let materials reveal recipes#

A carry rule can require any one type, every type, or a number in between. For example, a recipe using iron, leather and linen can require any two of those types to be carried before it appears, alongside its source requirements.

The rule is about visibility. It checks for at least one of each counted type; crafting still requires the complete quantities. Craft groups bulk choices by the number of different ingredients so recipes with different costs can receive appropriate thresholds.

Choose the crafting cost#

Add or set one material and quantity across a filtered collection, or edit a single recipe's ingredient list. Search the active material catalog by name, EditorID, defining plugin or FormID, and restore the scanned source cost whenever you want to return to it.

Changing a cost keeps the chosen visibility mode and threshold. Carry rules use the current ingredient list, so adding or removing a type can also change which carried materials satisfy a rule.

Build a patch for your profile#

Craft stores choices as you apply them, then generates a separate CraftPatch.esp inside Craft Output. Enable the mod and plugin in MO2, place the plugin after the recipe changes it should override, and check the affected crafting station in Skyrim.

Source plugins remain intact. When the modlist or profile changes, rescan and rebuild against the new inputs. These guides cover installation, selecting recipes, visibility, costs, patch building and the profile-sharing details that matter when you maintain more than one setup.

Keep control of your choices#

The saved decision set belongs to the Craft installation; the generated output belongs to the MO2 instance. Back up your decisions when updating the tool and rebuild for the profile you intend to play. To return the game to its source recipes, disable the generated output.

Start with Install & launch if Craft is new to you, then follow Your first patch. The recipe and reference sections cover bulk work, file locations, diagnostics and troubleshooting as you need them.
